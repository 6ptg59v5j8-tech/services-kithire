PLEASE READ BEFORE BOOKING

Crew hire terms.

1. Enquiries and bookings

All crew enquiries are subject to availability. A booking is only confirmed once DBAVL has provided written confirmation of the agreed dates, times, duties and price.

2. Rates and working hours

Published rates are starting rates and are not a final quotation. The agreed quote will confirm the working hours, any minimum call, overtime, late finishes, weekend or additional charges before the booking is accepted.

3. Changes and cancellations

Please request changes or cancellations in writing as early as possible. Any cancellation charges, changes to booked hours or additional costs will be set out in the individual quotation or booking confirmation.

4. Travel and expenses

Travel, accommodation, parking, subsistence and other expenses are not included unless the quotation specifically says otherwise. Any agreed expenses will be confirmed before the booking.

5. Breaks and facilities

Break arrangements should be agreed in advance and must comply with applicable working-time and health and safety requirements. The client should make suitable welfare facilities available while crew are on site.

6. Site safety and responsibilities

The client or event organiser is responsible for providing safe site access, a suitable working environment, relevant site information and any required inductions or risk assessments. Crew may pause work where they reasonably consider conditions unsafe.

7. Duties and equipment

Crew duties must remain within the agreed scope and the individual's skills and experience. Any additional responsibilities or changes to the job must be agreed in advance.

8. Invoicing and payment

Payment terms and the invoicing arrangements will be stated in the quotation or invoice. Any queries about an invoice should be raised promptly.

These terms apply to crew hire enquiries and bookings only. The terms in your written quotation or booking confirmation will take precedence where they set out specific arrangements for a job.
